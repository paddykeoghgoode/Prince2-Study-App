# PRINCE2 Revision Studio v15 - Foundation research and coverage notes

Updated: 2 November 2026

## What has been added

- **124 new, independently written PRINCE2 Project Management Foundation (Version 7.2) questions**, numbered `RX15-001` through `RX15-124`.
- Each question contains a specific, separately written explanation for **all four options**, an answer key, a topical tag and a PRINCE2 7.2 syllabus-criterion reference.
- Included question types: **113 standard, 5 two-correct-statement list, 2 missing-word and 4 negative** questions. Negative questions are intentionally uncommon.
- The existing original practice bank increases from **576 to 700 questions**. Existing separately supplied samples remain separate and have not been reclassified as original.
- The new `Fresh Question Lab` offers topic and style filters, guided 10/20-question sessions, and timed practice (up to 60 questions at one minute per question). The **60-question new-bank mock** follows the official five-domain weighting: 2 project concepts, 5 principles, 8 people, 36 practices and 9 processes.
- The original question bank, Study Library, matching games, Exam Arcade, source PDF library, smart review and history are retained.

## Important version and copyright safeguards

- PRINCE2 7 uses **practices**, not the older **themes** terminology, and includes **sustainability** as the seventh aspect of project performance.
- The 2013 75-question paper supplied earlier is an older exam with different rules, terminology and copyrighted content. Its questions were **not duplicated** in this update.
- The 2025 PeopleCert Sample Paper 2 is **linked, not copied** into the app. Existing PeopleCert Sample Paper 1 from the user's earlier files remains in its separately labelled mode.
- The 124 new items are **independently authored training questions**, not actual or leaked live exam questions and are not endorsed by PeopleCert.
- They are original questions based on the *concepts and coverage* indicated by the official syllabus, supplemented by research. Their exact wordings do not come from the external question banks.

## Exam preparation suggestion

Begin with one 10-question guided session. For each wrong choice, read what that choice actually means. Review the relevant Study Library chapter. Then take a 20-question timed test, and only later take the 60-question closed-book practice with the five-domain weighting. Use smart review for mistakes and confident guesses.
