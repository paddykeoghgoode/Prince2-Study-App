## Exam context

The current Foundation exam is **60 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes**, with **36/60** needed to pass. The study app simulates 20-, 30-, 40- and 60-question tests; only 60 questions/60 minutes is the official format.

For a 60-question syllabus-weighted mock, the app samples **2 project concepts, 5 principles, 8 People, 36 practices and 9 processes**.

## Licensing and quality

The published research set contains original training questions, **not official or leaked questions**, and is not endorsed by PeopleCert. This public repository excludes copyrighted course PDFs, restricted practice-paper text, extracted workbooks and private signing credentials. Some questions are educational approximations and should be checked against authorised training materials before formal use.
